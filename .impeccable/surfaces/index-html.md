## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the lumbre, the wood fire itself, burning live, with the olla de barro in it. It refuses the restaurant template of a stock food hero, a card grid and a map.

OWN-WORLD:
- Palette: soot (hollín) brown-black ground, and barro terracotta fields owning whole sections.
- Ember orange only as live light, ash white (ceniza) text, and the logo's chili red for the call action.
- Type: a heavy warm grotesk (Bricolage Grotesque) set big and tight, plus a plain body cut of the same family.
- Edges are clay-fired: round-bellied shapes come from the cazuela cut-outs, never card boxes.

STORY: The visitor sees that the fire is real and the pot is in it. They learn what comes out of it (the encargo dishes, the daily menu, the brasa and the guisos), believe the neighbours (4,6 · 229), and call.
