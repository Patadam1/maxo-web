## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static single-file HTML/CSS/JS on GitHub Pages, same as the owner's other sites (bezeta-web, hartielectrocool-web). No build step, easy to edit by hand.

## Users

Neighbours of Pedro Muñoz (Ciudad Real) and people from nearby towns and the road who are deciding where to eat today, mostly on a phone, often from Google Maps or Instagram. A second group plans ahead: they want to order a slow wood-fire dish (cocido, fabada, arroz con bogavante) by encargo for a family meal.

## Product Purpose

The website of Bar-Restaurante Maxó, "Comida Casera Cocinada en Leña". It should make people want to come and get them to phone and book a table or order a dish by encargo. Success means calls to 926 82 15 30.

## Positioning

The cooking happens over a real wood fire, in clay pots (olla de barro) set right in the embers. Slow dishes are made to order. That is a mechanism, not an adjective. A neighbouring bar cannot honestly claim it.

## Operating Context

- Open every day, 9:00–24:00, at C. Paloma 72, 13620 Pedro Muñoz.
- Large terrace.
- Dine in, takeaway, no delivery.
- Typical price is €10–20 per person.
- Bar culture applies: a tapa comes with the beer.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Primary action is a phone call to 926 82 15 30 (the landline confirmed by the owner). No online booking system.
- Encargo dishes need to be ordered ahead by phone.
- **Menu is provisional:** probably a menú del día, not confirmed. The page must hold it in an easy-to-edit data block.
- The old site maxocomidacasera.es (WordPress by Conecta6, Kit Digital) is no longer accessible to the owner, and the new site replaces it. The domain is undecided.
- Its stale data (phone 617 69 97 56, hours 06:00–22:30) must not be used.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Maxó.
- Tagline: "Comida Casera cocinada en Leña".
- Logo: dark grey MAXÓ wordmark with a red clay-pot-and-flame mark replacing the Ó, plus a red tagline (assets/logo-original.png, assets/logo-web-antigua.png).
- Voice: warm, plain and local, never fancy.

## Product Principles

1. Show the fire. The wood fire and the clay pot are the proof, so lead with them.
2. Calling is the one action. The number is always one tap away on mobile.
3. Truth over polish: only real dishes, real rating and real hours, with provisional content clearly marked for the owner.
4. Built for a phone in a hand on the street.
